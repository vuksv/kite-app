GoPro cross-check

Pick one or more original GoPro MP4 clips (HERO5 or newer). Only the clip's built-in telemetry is read, on this device; the video is never uploaded. The page finds the KiteLog session the clip belongs to, lists the KiteLog jumps that happen in the clip with their time in the video so you can scrub to them, and compares the two motion sensors.

Use the original file (GoPro Quik "Save" to Files, or the camera's SD card). Videos exported through the photo library are re-encoded without telemetry. GPS on the camera (Preferences › Regional › GPS) gives the exact clock; without it the clip is matched to KiteLog by the pattern of hits and slams.
